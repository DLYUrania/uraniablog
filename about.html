A Little About Me

Hello! I'm Linyan Duan, aka Urania, a postgraduate student at the University of Leeds, currently exploring the complicated relationship between media, technology, and everyday life.

My academic background is in journalism, which probably explains why I have a habit of questioning how information is produced, presented, and interpreted. Studying digital media has given me even more things to question. How convenient.

Things That Occupy My Brain

Outside university, my interests tend to wander in several directions, sometimes simultaneously.

I enjoy sleeping, video games, my playlists, and collecting random pieces of information that may never become useful.

I also have a remarkable ability to turn a simple question into an unnecessarily complicated train of thought.

Occasionally, I take photographs of things I find interesting. Occasionally, I spend twenty minutes deciding whether those photographs are actually interesting. Such is life.

My brain is essentially a browser with too many tabs open. Some are academic. Some are creative. One is probably playing music, and I have no idea which one.

About This Portfolio

This website is a record of my experiments, discoveries, occasional frustrations, and reflections throughout the Digital Practice module.

Rather than presenting a perfectly polished collection of achievements, I hope to document the process of learning itself, including the moments when technology refuses to cooperate.

After all, making mistakes is part of learning. At least, that's what I tell myself when my CSS stops working.
